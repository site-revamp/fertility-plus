Treatment fees

Know what to ask about costs.

Understanding the financial side of treatment is an important part of making an informed decision. Fertility Plus publishes treatment packages and pricing information for patients to review.

View published fees ↗

Look beyond the headline price.

  • Check which consultations, scans and blood tests are included.
  • Review medication, sedation and laboratory-related charges.
  • Ask about embryo, egg or sperm storage and future annual fees.
  • Clarify what happens if your treatment plan needs to change.
  • Request a clear explanation of any items not included in a package.
Ask about treatment costs ↗

Fees and package inclusions can change. Confirm current prices, exclusions and any additional charges directly with Fertility Plus before making a treatment decision.

Helpful information

Questions you may want to ask.

Start with the essentials. Your consultant can explain the details that apply to your medical history and personal circumstances.

Ask the clinic a question →
Where is Fertility Plus based?

Fertility Plus is based at 92 Harley Street, London, W1G 7HU. Consultations and monitoring take place at this location.

Can I arrange a video consultation?

Fertility Plus states that video consultations are available for new and existing patients. Contact the clinic to discuss suitability and appointment arrangements.

What happens at an initial fertility consultation?

Your consultant will discuss your circumstances and medical history, and may recommend investigations or assessments depending on your individual needs.

Where are assisted-conception procedures carried out?

Fertility Plus states that consultations and monitoring are based at 92 Harley Street, while assisted-conception procedures are carried out by the named consultant at Care Fertility. Confirm the arrangements for your proposed treatment with the clinic.

How can I understand the cost of treatment?

Review the published fees and ask the clinic to explain what is included, what may be charged separately and whether any additional costs could apply to your treatment plan.

Does everyone follow the same treatment plan?

No. Fertility investigations and treatment options depend on individual circumstances. A consultant can discuss the appropriate options, risks, benefits and alternatives with you.
