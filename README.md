# Fertility Plus – Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Fertility Plus is a private fertility and IVF clinic based on Harley Street, London, specialising in reproductive medicine and assisted conception. Its services include IVF, ICSI, IUI, fertility testing, egg and sperm freezing, donor treatment and fertility preservation.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating the clinic's personalised, consultant-led approach, improving treatment and pricing transparency, and helping prospective patients understand the treatment journey before making an enquiry.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer and More Transparent IVF Success Information
Improved the presentation of fertility success claims to emphasise clearly defined, verified outcomes and relevant supporting context. The revised approach addresses the lack of clarity around the previous claim of over 800 successfully treated women by prioritising accurate reporting, relevant patient groups and measurable outcomes rather than unsupported generalisations.

### 2. Simpler Treatment Pricing and Package Presentation
Reorganised the presentation of IVF packages, treatment costs, inclusions, exclusions and additional charges into a more accessible pricing structure. This addresses the difficulty of navigating extensive pricing information and helps prospective patients understand treatment costs and compare available options.

### 3. Clearer Patient Journey and Treatment Arrangements
Introduced a more structured explanation of the patient journey, covering:
- Initial consultation at Fertility Plus, Harley Street.
- Personalised investigations and treatment monitoring.
- Assisted-conception procedures at Care Fertility, performed by the named consultant.

This makes the distinction between consultation, monitoring and treatment procedures clearer, addressing uncertainty about where different stages of care take place.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual Hierarchy:** Strengthened the presentation of fertility services, clinical expertise, treatment information and primary enquiry opportunities.
- **Content Organisation:** Structured treatment categories, pricing information and patient journey content into clearer sections.
- **Pricing Presentation:** Improved the organisation of treatment packages and associated costs to make important financial information easier to understand.
- **Clinical Transparency:** Gave greater prominence to treatment arrangements and the distinction between Fertility Plus and Care Fertility.
- **User Experience:** Created a more logical progression from exploring fertility options to understanding costs and arranging a consultation.
- **Design Consistency:** Established a more cohesive presentation across typography, spacing, content sections and visual elements.
- **Conversion Focus:** Made consultation enquiries and relevant treatment information more accessible throughout the homepage.

## Project Objective

The redesigned homepage positions Fertility Plus as a personalised, consultant-led fertility clinic while addressing three important patient concerns: understanding clinical outcomes, knowing treatment costs and knowing what to expect throughout the treatment journey. The overall direction prioritises medical transparency, patient confidence and a clearer path towards an initial consultation.
